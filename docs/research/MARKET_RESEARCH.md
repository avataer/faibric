# Faibric Market Research: Finding the 5% That Generates 80% of Revenue

**Date:** January 8, 2026
**Purpose:** Identify lowest-hanging fruit niches with highest-paying customers that competitors CAN serve but DON'T focus on

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The AI app builder market is flooded with amateurs chasing volume. Lovable, Bolt, Base44 target hobbyists, broke founders, and developers who could use Cursor instead. They're selling $20/month subscriptions to people with no money.

**The opportunity:** Enterprise and growth-stage companies spend $50K-$500K+ on custom internal tools. They have:
1. **Budget** - IT budgets, VC funding, operational necessity
2. **Pain** - 71% of internal builds fail, 3-12 month IT backlogs
3. **Urgency** - Need solutions in weeks, not months

**Faibric's position:** Not competing with Lovable for hobbyists. Competing with $250K development agencies and 6-month timelines.

**Minimum price:** $100/month baseline, with per-app and per-hosting fees scaling to $500-$2,000/month for serious users.
