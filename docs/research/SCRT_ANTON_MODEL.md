# The SCRT Model (Anton's Framework)

**A comprehensive mental model for building companies that create real value through Flow State**

This document synthesizes insights from Peter Thiel (Zero to One), Alan Kay (Xerox PARC), Steve Jobs/Jony Ive (Apple), Eastern philosophy (Buddhism, meditation), and psychology into a unified framework for building differentiated companies.

---

## CORE PHILOSOPHY

### The Central Thesis

> "Not me but through me"

You are a conduit for creative energy, not the source. Ego blocks flow. The universe creates through you when you remove obstacles and align with proper intention.

**Key Insight:** Flow State is the only source of real value creation. All non-flow work derives from superficial sub-egos and their conflicts trapped in illusional beliefs. We see no value in transmitting such creations despite a superficial appearance of value.

---

## PART 1: FLOW STATE

### What is Flow?

Flow is when Ego freezes and attention focuses on the creative source. Mind and Flow never coexist - Mind is constructed to survive by adapting, not to create.

### Conditions for Flow

1. **Relaxation** (trust, safety, belief in self and universe)
2. **High energy level**, consistently maintained
3. **Focus on pleasure of the process** (not outcomes)
4. **Goals on the border of easy/impossible**
5. **Single intention** - only one goal at a time

### How to Achieve Flow

**1. Accumulate Energy:**
- Daily routine: food + exercise + relaxation
- Sport at least 3x/week in mornings
- No calories after 7-8pm
- Minimize sugar, plant-forward diet
- Exclude red meat, liquid calories
- Cool shower

**2. Remove Energy Losses:**
- Block people who drain you
- Get rid of things you aren't happy with
- Create comfort in working and relaxation spaces
- Transform sub-egos that waste energy

**3. Focus on Intention:**
- Daily journal of lessons and flow
- Meditation and visualization practice
- Intention is the only way to navigate flow

**4. Notice Signs from Reality:**
- Solutions to niche problems others don't want to solve
- Generate karma through valuable social relations
- React to signs, don't force outcomes

### What Blocks Flow

| Blocker | Why It Kills Flow |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Ego** | Wastes energy on dualities: "I want but I can't", "Good/bad", "Me/Them" |
| **Mind** | Works with old maps (wrong schemes) instead of direct reality |
| **Importance** | All "important" states are victim's states |
| **Tension** | Muscle tension blocks energy; always relax |
| **Goals focus** | Desire of results steals energy from process |
| **Too hard tasks** | Create tension which prevents flow |

### The Energy Equation

> Energy = Focus × Attention

If you spray energy across features, key features will lack value. Transform energy into products - that's the only real work.

## PART 2: FIRST PRINCIPLES THINKING

### The Thiel Questions

1. **"What important truth do very few people agree with you on?"**
2. **"What valuable company is nobody building?"**

Value is hidden in questions that are:
- Hard to answer (require hard-to-obtain PoV + courage)
- Important + Unknown
- Hard + Doable now

### The Secrets Framework

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Conventions** | Easy, crowded, not valuable |
| **Secrets** | Hard but doable - where value lives |
| **Mysteries** | Impossible |

**Social powers against secrets:**
1. Incrementalism ("Don't be too talented")
2. Fear of Risk (secrets are never vetted by mainstream)
3. Elites want stability, oppose secrets
4. Flatness ("Many are smarter than me, they'd have found it")

> "Those who don't force themselves to search for secrets don't see beyond conventions."

### PoV (Point of View) as Competitive Advantage

> "PoV is worth +100 IQ Points" - Alan Kay

- PoV change doubles intellect
- Shift PoV so restrictions become exactly what's needed
- Find PoV where your limitations are features, not bugs

**How to earn PoV:**
1. Get to the high ground (show up where changes happen)
2. Learn through work, go upwind (Math + Psychology > Economics)
3. First learn things that appear too difficult
4. Look for groups keeping lists of Important Problems

## PART 3: MONOPOLY STRATEGY

### Why Only Monopolies Survive

> "Every business is successful exactly to the extent that it does something others cannot."

- No sense to attempt anything other than monopoly
- Good enough products lead to bankruptcy
- Death awaits in the pool of undifferentiated products
- Competition makes people hallucinate opportunities where none exist

### The Monopoly Formula

**Required Elements:**

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| **10x Better** | Tech or design - otherwise impossible to sell |
| **Small Niche** | Start as the only solution for a specific niche |
| **Sequence** | Have a bold plan to dominate niche after niche |
| **Durability** | Answer: "Will it exist in 20 years?" |

**Four Monopoly Characteristics:**
1. Proprietary Technology (difficult to replicate)
2. Network Effects
3. Brand
4. Economies of Scale

### The 25% Test

> Target a small concentrated group served by no competitors.
> Test: Can you get 25% of the niche in 90 days?

Any large market is a bad choice - it lacks a good starting point or is flooded with competitors turning profits to zero.

### Sequencing Markets

1. Create or dominate a niche market
2. Gradually expand into related, slightly broader markets
3. Sequencing correctly is underrated
4. Gradual expansion requires discipline

## PART 4: DISTRIBUTION & SALES

### Distribution is #1

> "Superior distribution can create a monopoly by itself. A superior product cannot."

Most companies fail because of bad distribution, not bad products.

### The Dead Zone

| Price Point | Sales Method | Warning |
|-------------|--------------|---------|
| <$100/client | Marketing/Viral | Works |
| $100-$10,000 | **DEAD ZONE** | Nothing works here |
| >$10,000/client | Personal Sales | Works |

**You must sell for less than $100 or more than $10,000 per client.**

### Press is Key for Everything

- Selling to press is necessary to sell to everyone else
- Anyone worth hiring googles press first
- Any investor googles press before talking to you
- Any influential client googles before engaging

**Press Positioning:**
- Underdogs challenging status quo
- Status quo exists in interests of elites profiting from current unfair situation
- Build founder myth highlighting "not normal, not easily grouped"

### Viral Growth Requirements

- Short viral cycle
- From 1st session they naturally send compelling invites
- 25% of niche in 90 days can only come from Press or Viral + Press

## PART 5: TEAM & CULTURE

### The Conspiracy Model

> "A startup is a team on a mission - a conspiracy around a secret to change the future."

- 0-1 team is a conspiracy around a secret
- Small team is optimal size to conspire
- Largest group you can convince of a plan to build different future

### Hiring Principles

**The 21st Employee Question:**
> "Why will the 21st member join your team?"

Stock, smart teammates, and important problems are the worst answers - Google makes these same claims.

**Good answers are:**
1. About your Mission - why you're doing something important nobody else will get done
2. About your Team - why your company is a unique match for them personally

**Requirements:**
- Must envision long-term future together (not transactional)
- Must be excited about working specifically with you
- From outside, everyone should be different in the same way
- Everyone must have only 1 task at a time

### Culture

> "Culture eats strategy for breakfast"

- Culture is what you DO, not what you say
- No company "has" a culture - it IS a culture
- First 20 employees' lowest-quality actions = the culture

**Culture Building:**
- Pick virtues that help accomplish vision
- Virtues are actions, not just beliefs
- Focus on contrarian virtues nobody else follows
- Must have constant contact to sustain culture (i.e., focused breakfasts)
- Clear signals what is your culture and what is out of it

### Leadership

> "Top leaders are not imperialists, they are servants."

- Leading is serving something larger than you
- Coach tells what you don't want to hear so you can become who you want to become
- Build trust first, then give feedback
- Bet on people vs logic

## PART 6: PSYCHO-SOCIOLOGICAL NICHE

### Understanding Customer Psychology

**People want:**
- A magical feeling
- Ego goals achieved
- Design memories
- To express ideology through product choice

> "I use X because it states the ideology I want to think of myself as a supporter of"

**Examples:** Apple, Mercedes, Armani, Starbucks

### The Imitation Insight (Girard's Mimesis)

Mimesis (imitation) is the core ritual of people. Model designates desire - a friend or rival must desire something for you to desire it.

**Product Implications:**
- Relatability drives adoption
- Relatable design is 100x cheaper when designer makes for their own group
- Stories crafted to become what customers tell friends as their own

### Target Audience

> "Trying to please everyone will please nobody."

Focus on:
- Rebels, pirates, those challenging the current thing
- They consider themselves different than 99%
- People who value what you value

**The 99.9% Problem:**
Most companies (like most people) transmit the 5 base personality disorders, not values. This makes values-based companies wanted.

## PART 7: THE FOUNDER

### Founder as Myth Engine

> "Founders actually differ -> Extreme traits get picked up in PR -> Founders deepen extreme traits -> Cycle repeats"

**The Self-Manufactured Myth:**
1. Actual difference exists
2. Grow the extreme traits
3. Themselves exaggerate (press, posts)
4. Others exaggerate

**Key:** Myths are only about the extremes. Employ the extremes engine.

### Founder Paradox

Successful founders were simultaneously insiders AND outsiders. Build personal brand highlighting:
- Combination of opposites
- How they're not normal
- Underdogs

> "The most important task in business - the creation of new value - can't be reduced to a formula and applied by professionals."

### Monarchies vs. Democracies

> "Companies that create new tech often resemble feudal monarchies rather than modern organizations."

- Only monarchies can plan for decades
- Make non-obvious, non-democratic decisions
- Inspire and reward 10-year personal loyalty
- Bring out the best work from everybody

## PART 8: PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

### Design Values

| Value | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Elegance | Pure, clean, simple |
| MA (Japan) | Emptiness, space |
| Light (Buddhist) | Non-attachment |
| Giving a damn | Care through product |

> "Details manifest intention."

### Product Principles

1. **Less is More** - Fewer features but better
2. **We fill space because we can, not because we should**
3. **Good enough is not good enough**
4. **10x or nothing** - Incremental = no improvement for customer

### Avoiding Common Traps

| Trap | Reality |
|------|---------|
| "Lean Startup" / MVP | Making small changes leads to local maximum, not global maximum |
| Iteration | Don't iterate - plan and execute the plan |
| Disruption | A concept for incumbents; harmful as startup's PoV |
| Trends | "When you hear something that sounds like a trend - run away" |

### Value Creation Formula

**Value comes from:**
1. Amount of Value from Designed Experience
2. Amount of Value from Technological Advancement
3. Amount of Value from Fast Onboarding of friends

**The Thiel Test for Value:**
1. Create value
2. Last (durability)
3. Capture % of this value

## PART 9: PRACTICAL METHODS

### Decision Making

> "Illusion of choice. Courage to make a choice. Solutions look naturally obvious."

- Most alternatives are illusional fantasies wasting focus
- Mind understands through analogy with old solutions only
- Consciousness understands through direct visions

### Working with Reality

**Facts vs. Interpretations:**
- Facts: changes in physical world; emotions
- Mind's 24/7 work is interpreting reality which doesn't need to be interpreted
- Work with facts, address interpretations separately

### Alerts System

| Alert | Response |
|-------|----------|
| **Suffering Alert** | Stop! What's going on? Facts/Interpretations? Address reality. |
| **Importance Alert** | Important states are victim's states. Dissolve with humor. |
| **Muscle Tension Alert** | Tension always bad. Relax. Relaxation + Determination. |
| **Energy Leak Alert** | Tasks you resist? People who drain? Undigested emotions? |

### The Third Position

When conflicts arise:
1. Observe from neutral Third Position
2. What's the goal of ego #1 and #2?
3. Find win-win-win
4. Integrate conflicting PoVs into singular realizable positive intention

### The Ultimate Questions

1. **What great company is nobody building?**
2. **What does the greatest company ever possible look like?**
3. **Why are we not building it right now?**

### Success Indicators

- Company's Revenue exceeds Spendings on 6-month projected period
- Revenue comes from products designed, built, and delivered according to declared values
- Flow State activities are the priority
- Growth brings deep satisfaction

### The Final Truth

> "The empty nature of all things and events is the only truth."

**Two truths about future:**
1. Will be different
2. Is rooted in today

Our challenge is to imagine and create new and better ways of doing things.

## SUMMARY: THE SCRT MODEL IN ONE PAGE

### Core Belief
Flow State is the only source of real value. Ego blocks flow. Remove obstacles, align intention, let creation happen through you.

### Strategy
Build a monopoly by finding secrets (hard truths few agree with), dominating a small niche (25% in 90 days), then sequencing to adjacent markets. Only 10x improvements matter.

### Distribution
Either sell for <$100 (viral/marketing) or >$10,000 (personal sales). Press is key for everything. Build founder myth around extremes and opposites.

### Team
Conspiracy around a secret. Only hire people who can't imagine not joining. Culture is what you do, not what you say. Leaders serve.

### Product
Less is more. Details manifest intention. No MVP iteration - plan and execute. Avoid trends. Good enough = bankruptcy.

### Execution
One task at a time. Facts not interpretations. Third position for conflicts. Relax + Determine. Trust the process, not outcomes.
