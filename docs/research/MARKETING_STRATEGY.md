**Date:** January 8, 2026
**Purpose:** Find profitable niche that doesn't compete directly with Lovable, v0, Bolt, Base44

---

## 1. MARKET REALITY: CAC & ECONOMICS

### Customer Acquisition Costs (2025-2026)

| Channel | CAC | Notes |
|---------|-----|-------|
| Google Ads (SaaS) | $802 | Tech keywords $3-8+ CPC |
| SEO (Organic) | $290-$480 | Takes 6+ months to build |
| Referrals | $150 | Lowest CAC, highest trust |
| Meta Ads | $500-$800 | Visual-first, good for demos |

**Key Ratio:** CLV:CAC must be 3:1 minimum
- If CAC = $500, customer must generate $1,500+ lifetime value
- At $20/month, that's 75 months (6+ years) to break even
- **Conclusion:** $20/month pricing doesn't work with paid ads

### What This Means for Faibric

To profitably buy customers via Google/Meta ads:
- **$50/month pricing** = $600/year, CAC must be <$200
- **$100/month pricing** = $1,200/year, CAC can be ~$400
- **One-time $500 purchase** = CAC must be <$170

**OR:** Don't compete on paid ads. Use content/SEO/referrals.

## 3. UNDERSERVED NICHES (Opportunities)

### A. Agency/Freelancer White-Label (HIGH POTENTIAL)

**The Gap:** Lovable, v0, Bolt are all B2C. No major player owns B2B2C (building for clients).

**Target Audience:**
- Web agencies (10,000+ in US alone)
- Freelance developers on Upwork/Fiverr
- Marketing agencies adding "app development" to services
- Consultants who need to deliver client tools

**Why They're Underserved:**
- Lovable/Bolt = client sees the builder (not white-label)
- Buildfire/Adalo = mobile-first, not web apps
- No AI-first white-label web app builder exists

**Positioning:** "The AI app builder for agencies. Build client apps 10x faster, charge full price."

**Economics:**
- Freelancers charge $300-$1,000 per internal tool
- Agencies charge $5,000-$50,000 per client app
- They'd pay $100-$200/month for a tool that lets them deliver faster

**Ad Keywords (Lower Competition):**
- "white label app builder"
- "app builder for agencies"
- "build apps for clients"
- "freelance app development tools"

---

### B. Vertical-Specific Builders (MEDIUM POTENTIAL)

**The Gap:** Generic builders struggle with industry-specific needs.

**Verticals to Consider:**

| Vertical | Pain Point | App Types |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Real Estate | Listing tools, CRM, client portals | Property showcase, booking |
| Legal | Document automation, client intake | Contract generators, intake forms |
| Healthcare | HIPAA compliance, patient tools | Appointment booking, intake |
| Fitness | Scheduling, member management | Booking, workout tracking |
| Restaurants | Ordering, reservations | Menu apps, ordering systems |

**Positioning:** "The AI app builder for [VERTICAL]. Pre-built templates, industry compliance, launch in hours."

**Economics:**
- Vertical SaaS commands premium pricing
- Less price sensitivity than generic tools
- Higher LTV due to switching costs

**Challenge:** Requires deep vertical knowledge, compliance, integrations.

---

### C. Local Business Internal Tools (MEDIUM POTENTIAL)

**The Gap:** "Previously un-venture-backable" markets now viable.

**Target Audience:**
- Pet stores tracking inventory
- Therapists managing intake forms
- Cleaning services tracking jobs
- Contractors managing quotes
- Small shops with spreadsheet chaos

**Why They're Underserved:**
- Too small for Salesforce/enterprise tools
- Too specific for generic no-code
- Can't afford custom development

**Positioning:** "Turn your spreadsheet chaos into a real app. $29/month."

**Economics:**
- Low CAC via local SEO, Google Maps, Facebook Groups
- High volume, low touch
- Upsell to "done for you" service

**Challenge:** Support burden, low revenue per customer.

---

### D. "Vibe Coding" for Specific Outcomes (HIGH POTENTIAL)

**The Insight:** Generic "build anything" loses to specific outcomes.

**Outcome-Specific Positioning:**

| Outcome | Audience | Message |
|---------|----------|---------|
| "Build a client portal" | Freelancers, consultants | "Give clients a branded portal in 10 minutes" |
| "Build an internal dashboard" | Ops managers | "Turn your data into a dashboard today" |
| "Build a booking system" | Service businesses | "Stop using Calendly. Own your booking app." |
| "Build a simple CRM" | Solo founders | "A CRM that's actually simple. Built by AI." |

**Why This Works:**
- Specific = findable (SEO, ads)
- Specific = believable (not "build anything")
- Specific = higher conversion (clear value prop)

## 4. RECOMMENDED STRATEGY: AGENCY-FIRST

### Why Agency-First Wins

1. **Higher willingness to pay** - Agencies have client budgets
2. **Lower CAC** - Smaller target market, more targeted ads
3. **Natural virality** - Agencies show work to other agencies
4. **Recurring revenue** - Monthly retainer for tool access
5. **No direct competition** - Lovable/Bolt don't serve this
6. **White-label = lock-in** - Hard to switch once branded

### Faibric for Agencies - Product Requirements

| Feature | Priority | Why |
|---------|----------|-----|
| White-label (remove Faibric branding) | Critical | Agencies sell as their own |
| Client handoff (transfer app ownership) | Critical | Deliver to client cleanly |
| Team seats | High | Agency has multiple builders |
| Custom domains per app | High | Professional delivery |
| Usage-based billing | High | Charge per app, not flat rate |
| Client preview links | High | Get approval before launch |
| Template library | Medium | Faster starting points |
| API access | Medium | Integrate with agency tools |

### Pricing Model for Agencies

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Freelancer | $49/month | 3 apps, white-label, 1 seat |
| Agency | $149/month | 15 apps, white-label, 5 seats |
| Agency Pro | $349/month | Unlimited apps, priority support, 15 seats |
| Enterprise | Custom | API, SSO, dedicated support |

**Per-App Hosting:** $9/month per deployed app (passed to client)

### Go-to-Market for Agencies

**Phase 1: Content & SEO (Months 1-3)**
- Blog: "How to add app development to your agency services"
- Blog: "Pricing guide: What to charge for client apps"
- Blog: "White-label app builders compared"
- YouTube: Build a client app in 10 minutes (demo)
- Target keywords: "white label app builder", "app builder for agencies"

**Phase 2: Community & Partnerships (Months 2-4)**
- Agency Facebook groups (DigitalMarketer, etc.)
- Upwork/Fiverr freelancer communities
- Partner with agency consultants
- Affiliate program (20% recurring)

**Phase 3: Targeted Ads (Month 4+)**
- LinkedIn Ads targeting agency owners
- Google Ads on "white label" keywords (lower CPC)
- Retargeting blog visitors
- Case study ads (social proof)

**Phase 4: Outbound (Month 5+)**
- Cold email to web agencies
- Agency directory outreach
- Conference sponsorships (smaller agency events)

## 7. UNIT ECONOMICS TARGET

### Agency Plan ($149/month)

**Revenue per customer:** $1,788/year

**Costs:**
- AI generation (15 apps × $0.95): ~$14/month
- Hosting overhead: ~$10/month
- Support (amortized): ~$20/month
- **Total cost:** ~$44/month

**Gross margin:** ~70%

**Target CAC:** <$500 (3:1 CLV:CAC)

**Payback:** ~4 months

### Per-App Hosting ($9/month per app)

**Revenue:** $9/month
**Cost (Vercel/Render):** ~$2-4/month
**Margin:** 50-75%

This is pure profit center - clients pay, Faibric margins.
